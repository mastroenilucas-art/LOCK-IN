How to run your week

3 daysA → B → C, with a rest day between when you can (Mon · Wed · Fri works). Back-to-back days are fine too.
2 daysTap Only 2 days at the top. Day C turns blue and splits: Pairs 1–2 go after Session A, Pairs 3–4 after Session B. Do them as long as you can, and split whatever's left into 10–15 min mini-sessions that week.
1 dayDo the session you did longest ago, in Short mode if you need it. A week like that keeps your muscle. It doesn't undo anything.
Week 9Deload: half the sets, same weights. Then start again at week 1 with your new numbers.

If this happens, do this

Under 40 minTap Short: Pairs 1–2 only, about 30 min.
Machine takenTap Busy under that exercise.
No gymTap No gym under each exercise. Same sets and reps.
Gym packedCan't hold two stations? Do each exercise on its own: 90 s between sets, 2 min on the heavy lifts.
Bad sleepUnder 6 h or wiped out: Short mode, every set 2–3 reps short. A short session still counts.
Lower backA twinge: stop the set and finish with the Busy version. Pain running down the leg, or still there next session: skip leg work and get it checked first.
Sharp painAnywhere, and not the burn: stop that exercise and use Busy. Still hurts: skip it and write it in Notes.
Rugby againDrop the last set of every leg exercise. Upper body stays the same.
